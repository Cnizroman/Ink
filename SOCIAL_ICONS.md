# Social icons

Brand SVG paths bundled into `profile-tools.js` come from
[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under
[CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).

- Version 16.36.0: Telegram, VK, YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, Discord, Twitch,
  X and Facebook.
- Version 11.15.0: LinkedIn.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. These icons identify
external services; they do not imply endorsement or a verified connection.
Icons render locally using `currentColor`, without a CDN or external image
requests. Only a fixed allowlist of SVG paths is used; user SVG is never rendered.
